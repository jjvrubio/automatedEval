
# ✅ Convercus Partner Program Kit  
*(Draft for Iberia Rollout – 12-Month Horizon)*

---

## 🎯 Program Purpose
Build a **partner-driven ecosystem** to accelerate Convercus’ adoption in Iberia by leveraging **digital agencies, CRM integrators, and loyalty specialists** as **trusted intermediaries**.

---

## 🔑 Partnership Models

| **Tier**             | **Description**                                               | **Target Partners**              |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Referral Partner** | Introduces opportunities to Convercus; earns commission      | Agencies without deep loyalty capability |
| **Reseller Partner** | Sells Convercus licenses; co-branded proposals              | Full-service agencies with strong sales |
| **Certified Implementer** | Delivers technical deployments, configures loyalty programs | CRM-focused agencies, Martech specialists |
| **Co-creation Partner** | Co-designs loyalty innovation frameworks and pilots       | Strategic consultancies, Jakala-level firms |

## 📂 Enablement Assets (Partner Hub Content)
**To deliver in Q1 (see roadmap):**
- **Sales Toolkit**: Pitch decks, ROI calculators, case studies.
- **Integration Guides**: Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP connectors.
- **API Documentation**: REST API reference and examples.
- **Certification Path**:
  - *Level 1*: Convercus Fundamentals
  - *Level 2*: Advanced Loyalty Orchestration
- **Marketing Kit**: Co-branded templates, success stories.

---

## 📈 Incentive Structure
- **Referral Commission**: % of net license value.
- **Reseller Margin**: Volume-based (10–20%).
- **Implementation Services**: Billed by partner with Convercus certification premium.
- **Annual Awards**: “Top Loyalty Innovator Iberia” recognition at Shoptalk.

---

## 📅 Activation Timeline (Aligned with Roadmap)
- **Q1**: Build Partner Hub; onboard first 10 agencies as Referral Partners.
- **Q2**: Certify 3–4 Implementers; launch first **agency-led webinar**.
- **Q3**: Roll out joint pilots; publish **success case studies**.
- **Q4**: Co-sponsor **Shoptalk Europe** and **Digital 1to1** with partners.
