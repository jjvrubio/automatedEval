# Commercial Review

Use this reference when reviewing pricing, ROI, scope, and approval status.

## Economic Controls

Check that every amount, discount, capacity, term, and scenario has a current source. Separate:

- Implementation and onboarding.
- Integrations and custom work.
- SaaS/subscription.
- Variable usage, transactions, messages, or rewards.
- Third-party services.
- Taxes and pass-through costs.
- Optional modules and exclusions.

Do not calculate ROI unless the opportunity provides the necessary margin contribution, cost basis, incentives, and scenario assumptions. Do not infer missing values from Compactor or another account.

## Approval Separation

Report three states independently:

- `Word generation`: whether the DOCX was produced without unresolved conversion errors.
- `Documentary review`: whether the document is internally coherent, complete, and free from visible source/control issues.
- `Commercial approval`: whether an authorized commercial owner has approved the economics and terms.

A generated Word document is not automatically reviewed or commercially approved.

## Client-Ready Gate

Do not label an offer client-ready if any of these remain:

- Pending validated pricing or final scope.
- Internal costs, markup, OCR strategy, negotiation notes, or supplier margin.
- Contradictory assumptions or exclusions.
- Unresolved legal, tax, privacy, integration, or service-level conditions.
- Placeholder fields or stale dates/versions.
